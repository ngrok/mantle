New migration guides are named by slug, with no number in the file name or the URL. A new guide lives at `https://mantle.ngrok.com/migrations/<slug>-migration`, and its codemod ships at `node_modules/@ngrok/mantle/dist/migrations/<slug>.yml`. Each release gives the guides it ships the next numbers, and the migrations index shows that number. Guides 0001 to 0035 keep their URLs and codemod paths.

Charts keep their focus indicator and their cursor in forced-colors mode (Windows High Contrast). The focused chart and the focused scrollable `Legend` show an outline, where before the focus ring, a box shadow, was removed and nothing replaced it. The crosshair paints in the system highlight color, the hover band gets an outline in that color, and the hover dots and the tooltip's series key keep their series color.

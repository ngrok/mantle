`AppLayout` gains a side panel, its state owner and trigger, cookie persistence for it, and a width cap for wide screens.

`AppLayout.SidePanel` (`data-slot="app-layout-side-panel"`) is a second card after `AppLayout.Content`, for an assistant, a details pane, or any surface that opens beside the page and closes again. It renders an `<aside>`, the `complementary` landmark, with the same card treatment as the content card, and reads like a `Sheet` with no focus trap and no backdrop. Three parts fill it: `AppLayout.SidePanelHeader` (`data-slot="app-layout-side-panel-header"`), the toolbar row on the same band as `AppLayout.Header` so the two rows center on one line; `AppLayout.SidePanelBody` (`data-slot="app-layout-side-panel-body"`), the scroll region, the same box as `AppLayout.Body` with no landmark; and an optional `AppLayout.SidePanelFooter` (`data-slot="app-layout-side-panel-footer"`), a bar pinned to the bottom edge outside the scroll region.

`AppLayout.SidePanelRoot` owns whether the panel is open, the way `Sidebar.Root` owns the rail: `open` + `onOpenChange` controlled, or `defaultOpen` uncontrolled, closed by default. It is renderless; wrap `AppLayout.Workspace` with it. `AppLayout.SidePanelTrigger` (`data-slot="app-layout-side-panel-trigger"`) is the ghost `IconButton` that toggles it, with `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`, `data-state`, and a tooltip with its label, the same shape as `Sidebar.Trigger`; it needs a `TooltipProvider` ancestor. `useSidePanel()` returns `open`, `setOpen`, `toggle`, and `panelId` for a custom control. When the panel closes while focus is inside it, the root moves focus to the first trigger outside the panel.

Open, the panel takes `--app-layout-side-panel-width` beside the card (`clamp(21rem, 24vw, 27rem)`) and the card absorbs the difference. Closed, it collapses to nothing and goes `inert`, with its children still mounted so a conversation keeps its scroll position and its in-flight reply across a close and a reopen. The change animates `flex-basis` alone over 200ms on the new `--ease-out-strong` theme token (`cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)`), the way the rail collapses: the `<aside>` is a clip box and the card is an inner column pinned at the open width, so nothing inside reflows or moves, a second click mid-flight reverses the run, `motion-reduce:` turns it into a cut, and the transition holds until one frame after hydration (`data-hydrated`) so a state the client corrects after the server render snaps instead of sliding. Below `mobileBreakpoint` (`"sm"`, `"md"`, or `"lg"`, `"lg"` by default) the panel floats over the card and slides off the end edge when closed; one breakpoint lower it covers the card from gutter to gutter. The panel stamps `data-state="open" | "closed"` and `data-hydrated`.

`extractSidePanelStateCookie`, `serializeSidePanelStateCookie`, and `SIDE_PANEL_STATE_COOKIE_NAME` (`"mantle-side-panel-state"`) persist the state to a cookie the server can read, the same pair the sidebar ships: read the cookie in the loader into `defaultOpen`, and write it from `onOpenChange`, so the server HTML carries the right state with no first-frame correction.

`AppLayout.Workspace` now caps at `--app-layout-max-width` (`128rem`, 2048px) and centers in the frame, so an ultrawide monitor gets a centered shell instead of a card stretched across it. The cap is the width at which a 13rem rail, a `max-w-7xl` page with its padding, and an open side panel at its 27rem cap are each at full size. `AppLayout.Root` still paints `bg-base` edge to edge, and `AppLayout.Notice` still spans the full window. Set the variable to `none` on `AppLayout.Root` to let the row fill any width, and merge `className="ml-0"` on the workspace to pin the row to the start edge. The workspace is also `relative` and `min-w-0`, so a floating side panel resolves against the row and a nested row cannot push the panel off the window.
